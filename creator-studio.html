Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All four studios are free with unlimited downloads and no watermark.

Do I need to install anything?

No. They run in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on your phone or computer; the video is encoded on your device.

How do I put the green-screen clips on my video?

Add the MP4 as an overlay in CapCut, InShot, Premiere or DaVinci and apply Chroma key to remove the green.

Which format should I pick?

9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 16:9 for YouTube; 1:1 for feed posts.
