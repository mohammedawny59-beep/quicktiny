Milestones

Only about 1 in 25 babies arrive on the due date — most come within two weeks either side. An early ultrasound gives the most accurate date; follow your doctor or midwife.

How the due date is calculated

  • Naegele's rule: first day of the last period + 280 days (40 weeks).
  • Longer or shorter cycles move the date: a 32-day cycle adds 4 days.
  • From conception: + 266 days (38 weeks).

Frequently asked questions

Why do weeks count from my last period?

It's the standard medical convention — the first two “weeks” happen before conception.
