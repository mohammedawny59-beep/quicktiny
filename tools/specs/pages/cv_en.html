Tips for a CV that gets interviews

  • One or two pages. Recruiters skim; put your strongest, most recent work first.
  • Start bullets with a verb and a number: “Cut reporting time from 3 days to 2 hours” beats “Responsible for reports”.
  • Match the job ad. Use the same skill names the posting uses so ATS filters find you.
  • Skip the photo, age and marital status unless the employer or country expects them.
