How to add the captions to your video

  • Got a subtitle file? Export .srt from CapCut, Premiere or YouTube Studio and upload it here — every word keeps its timing.
  • No subtitles yet? Paste your script, set the speaking speed and nudge it until it matches your voice.
  • Download the MP4, add it as an overlay above your video and apply Chroma key to remove the green. Line the start up with your first word.
  • Use 2–3 words on screen for fast Reels and 4 for slower talking videos.

Frequently asked questions

Does it transcribe my audio automatically?

Not yet — it animates captions from your text or an .srt file. Most editors (CapCut, YouTube Studio) can auto-generate an .srt you can upload here.

Is it free with no watermark?

Yes, unlimited free downloads with no watermark.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic words run right-to-left with proper letter joining, and the karaoke highlight moves from right to left.

Is my script uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything — including the video encoding — happens on your device.
