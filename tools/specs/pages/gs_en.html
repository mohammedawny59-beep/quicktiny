Tips for a polished result

  • Keep each slide short: a title of 2–5 words and one supporting line reads best on a phone.
  • 60 fps gives the smoothest motion (Instagram and TikTok keep it); choose 30 fps for smaller files.
  • Use your own photo as the background for a branded look; the glass blurs it automatically behind the text.
  • Between 2 and 3 seconds per slide feels calm; under 2 seconds feels energetic.
