How the count works

  • By default the start date counts and the end date doesn't (like “nights” on a trip). Tick “include the end date” to count both.
  • Working days skip the weekend you choose — Saturday/Sunday or Friday/Saturday. Public holidays aren't removed.
  • Leap years are handled automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How many days until New Year?

Set the start date to today and the end date to January 1 — the result updates instantly.

What date is 90 days from today?

Use “Add or subtract days”: from today, add 90.
