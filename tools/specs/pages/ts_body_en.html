    How it works

    • Best quality that fits — it searches for the highest JPEG quality under your limit instead of guessing, so your photo stays as sharp as possible.
    • Shrinks only when needed — if a photo can't fit even at lower quality, it's scaled down step by step, keeping its proportions.
    • Safe for every form — “100 KB” is treated as 100,000 bytes, so the file is under the limit whether the site counts 1,000 or 1,024 bytes per KB.
    • Private — ID photos and documents never leave your device.

    Common size limits

    • Passport and visa photos on many portals: 50–240 KB.
    • Job and exam applications: often 20–100 KB for photos and 10–50 KB for signatures.
    • Email-friendly photos: 200–500 KB.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will my photo look bad at 20 KB?

    It's scaled to a smaller size so it stays clear — a 20 KB photo is usually around 300–450 px wide, which is what most forms expect anyway.

    The form also needs exact dimensions — what do I do?

    Fill in max width and height (for example 200 × 230). The photo is fitted inside that box first, then compressed to the size limit.

    Can I compress a PDF to 100 KB?

    Use Compress PDF — it works best on scanned documents.
