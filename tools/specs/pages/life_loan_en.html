Flat rate vs reducing balance

  • Reducing balance (APR): interest is charged only on what you still owe, so it shrinks every month.
  • Flat rate: interest is charged on the original amount for the entire term. A 3% flat rate over 5 years costs about the same as 5.6% APR.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months.
