How the countdown works

  • Before Ramadan it counts down to the start of 1 Ramadan in your city's time zone (Umm al-Qura calendar).
  • During Ramadan it switches automatically: to iftar (Maghrib) during the day and to the end of suhoor (Fajr) at night.
  • Prayer times use each city's official method (Umm al-Qura for Saudi Arabia, Egyptian authority for Egypt, and so on).

Frequently asked questions

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

On the Umm al-Qura calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 AH falls on Monday 8 February 2027, so the first tarawih is on the evening of 7 February. The official start depends on the moon sighting.

When is iftar today?

Iftar is at Maghrib. Pick your city and the page shows today's Maghrib time and a live countdown to it during Ramadan.

When does suhoor end?

Suhoor ends at Fajr. Many timetables show imsak about 10 minutes earlier as a precaution.

Is my location sent anywhere?

No. You choose a city from the list and everything is calculated in your browser.
