Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y. Example: 15% of 200 = 30.
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Example: 30 of 200 = 15%.
  • Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. From 80 to 100 = +25%; from 100 to 80 = −20%.
  • Discount: price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). 200 with 25% off = 150.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a 20% drop not undone by a 20% rise?

Because the second percentage is taken from a smaller number: 100 − 20% = 80, and 80 + 20% = 96. To get back to 100 you need +25%.

How do I calculate VAT?

Use “add a percentage” with your VAT rate, or the dedicated VAT calculator (Arabic) for invoices and extracting VAT from a total.
