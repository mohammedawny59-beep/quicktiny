How are these prayer times calculated?

  • From the sun's position for your coordinates and date, using the method's Fajr and Isha angles (e.g. Umm al-Qura: Fajr 18.5°, Isha 90 minutes after Maghrib, 120 in Ramadan).
  • Official minute adjustments are included where countries publish them (Jordan, UAE, Morocco, Turkey).
  • Checked against a widely used reference service: within 0–2 minutes for most cities and methods (Asr can differ by up to 3 minutes at high latitudes).
  • Imsak is shown 10 minutes before Fajr. Always follow your local mosque or official authority if they differ.

Frequently asked questions

Which method should I choose?

The tool picks your country's usual method automatically. Saudi Arabia uses Umm al-Qura, Egypt the Egyptian Authority, North America ISNA, and most of Europe the Muslim World League.

Does it need my location?

Only if you tap “Use my location” — the coordinates stay in your browser. You can just pick a city instead.
