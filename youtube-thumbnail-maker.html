Tips for thumbnails that get clicks

  • Use 3–5 big words. The thumbnail should add to the title, not repeat it.
  • A face with a clear emotion gets more clicks — the background remover cuts you out and adds a white sticker outline.
  • Highlight one number or key word in a bright colour.
  • Keep the bottom-right corner clear: YouTube covers it with the video length.
  • Check the small previews — if you can’t read it at phone size, make it shorter.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280×720 pixels (16:9), JPG or PNG, under 2 MB. This maker exports exactly that and lowers JPG quality automatically if the file would be too big.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Background removal runs on your device with a small AI model; your photo never leaves your browser.

Does it support Arabic titles?

Yes — Arabic is written right-to-left in a bold Arabic display font and the layout mirrors itself.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no watermark and no sign-up.
