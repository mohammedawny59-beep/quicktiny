Fancy Name Generator for PUBG, Free Fire & Fortnite

Type your gamer tag and get dozens of stylish versions — 𝓢𝓬𝓻𝓲𝓹𝓽, ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ, 𝕲𝖔𝖙𝖍𝖎𝖈, bubbles and framed names like 亗 Name 亗 or ꧁Name꧂. One tap to copy. Works for Arabic names too.

Style my name

How to use it in your game

  1. Type your name, then tap Copy on the style you like.
  2. In the game, open the rename option (PUBG Mobile and Free Fire need a rename card) and paste.
  3. If a symbol shows as a box or the game rejects it, pick a simpler style — each game supports a different set of characters.

Good to know

  • The character count on each style helps you stay under your game's name limit.
  • These are real Unicode characters, so they also work on Discord, Instagram, TikTok and Steam.
  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere — styles are generated in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my game show squares instead of the letters?

That game's font doesn't include those Unicode characters. Bold, small caps and framed styles are the most widely supported; try one of those.

Can I decorate Arabic names?

Yes — type an Arabic name and you'll get tatweel styles (مـحـمـد), harakat decorations and frames. There's also an Arabic version of this page.
