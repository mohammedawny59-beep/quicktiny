How zakat al-fitr is calculated

  • Zakat al-fitr is one sa' of staple food (rice, wheat, dates…) for each person you support, including children.
  • The sa' is usually estimated at about 2.5 to 3 kg depending on the food and the scholarly body.
  • Many countries announce an official cash amount per person each Ramadan for those who give money.
  • It should be given before the Eid prayer; many give it in the last days of Ramadan.

Frequently asked questions

How much is zakat al-fitr per person?

One sa' of staple food — about 2.5 to 3 kg. If you give cash, use the amount your country's official body announces for this year.

Who do I pay zakat al-fitr for?

For yourself and everyone you are responsible for supporting, such as your spouse and children, if you have food beyond your needs for the day of Eid.

When is the deadline?

Before the Eid al-Fitr prayer. Giving it a day or two before Eid is widely accepted.

What is fidya?

For someone who can't fast and won't be able to make the days up (e.g. chronic illness or old age), fidya is feeding one poor person for each day missed. Enter the days and the cost of a meal.
