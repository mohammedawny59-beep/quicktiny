How to use a template

  1. Tap a template above — the previews play live.
  2. Replace the text and add your photo where there is one.
  3. Pick colours (or save your brand kit in the Creator Studio to apply it automatically) and the format: 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 1:1 or 4:5 for feed, 16:9 for YouTube.
  4. Download the MP4. 60 fps looks extra smooth on phones.

Frequently asked questions

Are the templates free for commercial use?

Yes. The animations are generated by this page, so you can use the videos for your business or clients without attribution.

Can I add music?

Add music in CapCut, Instagram or TikTok after downloading — the MP4 is silent so you can pick a trending sound.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Photos stay in your browser and the video is rendered on your device.
