Paper Motion Studio — Paper-Cut Text & Image Animations for Reels

Type a word or drop a photo and get a scroll-stopping paper animation for Reels, TikTok and Shorts: newspaper clippings, cut-out letters, a photo that unfolds like paper, posts, a typewriter, a printed receipt, sticky notes or live marker handwriting. Works in Arabic and English. Export a vertical MP4 or a green-screen version for your editor — free, no watermark, nothing uploaded.

How to use it in your video

  • Pick a style, type your word (or drop a photo for Paper unfold), then press Download MP4.
  • Use Green screen to drop the animation over your own footage. Key out the green in CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci (Chroma key).
  • Keep the main word short, 1–3 words. Put the details in the second line or in your voice-over.
  • Every frame is drawn on your device, so the download is the exact animation you see.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free with no watermark?

Yes. Exports are free, unlimited and watermark-free.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic text is laid out right-to-left with proper letter joining, and cut-out letters split Arabic only where letters don't connect.

Which video format do I get?

MP4 (H.264, 1080×1920 at 30 fps) in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Browsers without H.264 encoding get WebM, which CapCut and Instagram also accept.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Background removal and rendering run in your browser.
