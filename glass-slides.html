Tips for a polished result

  • Keep each slide short: a title of 2–5 words and one supporting line reads best on a phone.
  • 60 fps gives the smoothest motion (Instagram and TikTok keep it); choose 30 fps for smaller files.
  • Use your own photo as the background for a branded look; the glass blurs it automatically behind the text.
  • Between 2 and 3 seconds per slide feels calm; under 2 seconds feels energetic.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free with no watermark?

Yes. Downloads are free, unlimited and watermark-free.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic slides run right-to-left with proper letter joining.

Why 60 fps?

Twice as many frames as standard video, so transitions and the moving background look silky, especially on newer phones.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your text and photo stay in your browser and the video is created on your device.
