    Why pixel width matters

    Google cuts titles by width, not by character count — "WWWW" takes far more room than "iiii". The meter measures your title in the same font and size Google uses on desktop (about 600 px), so you can see the cut-off before it happens.

    Tips for titles and descriptions that get clicks

    • Put the main keyword near the start of the title.
    • Give a reason to click: free, fast, a number, a result.
    • Keep descriptions around 120–155 characters and write them for people, not robots.
    • Use a 1200×630 share image — it fits Facebook, LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Google always use my description?

    No — Google sometimes writes its own snippet from your page when it fits the search better. A clear, relevant description makes it much more likely yours is used.

    Why didn't my share image import?

    For privacy, this page doesn't contact other websites. Upload the image file to preview it; the og:image URL is still added to your tags.
