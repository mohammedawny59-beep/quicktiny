Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert a Unix timestamp (seconds or milliseconds) to a readable date in your local time zone and UTC, or convert a date back to a timestamp.

Try it now

How to use Timestamp Converter

  1. Enter a Unix timestamp (in seconds or milliseconds) and click "Convert to date", or click "Use current time".
  2. Or pick a date and time and click "Convert to timestamp".
  3. Results show both your local time and UTC.

Key benefits

  • Auto-detects seconds vs. milliseconds.
  • Shows both local time and UTC side by side.
  • One click to grab the current timestamp.
  • Runs entirely in your browser using your device's clock.

Privacy

Timestamp Converter runs entirely in your browser using local JavaScript. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to QuickTiny's servers.

Frequently asked questions

How does it know if my timestamp is in seconds or milliseconds?

Timestamps larger than 20,000,000,000 are treated as milliseconds; smaller values are treated as seconds. This covers all realistic dates.

What time zone is used?

Your input date is interpreted in your browser's local time zone. The UTC value is shown alongside it for reference.

Can I convert dates before 1970?

Yes — negative Unix timestamps representing dates before January 1, 1970 are supported.
