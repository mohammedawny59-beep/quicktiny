The problem with product photo uploads

A new collection often means dozens of product photos at once — straight out of a camera or a photo editor, usually a few megabytes each. Uploading them one at a time to Shopify, resizing each in a separate app first, is slow and easy to put off. Large, uncompressed images also mean more data for a shopper's browser to download on every product and collection page.

How it works

  1. Pay once for QuickTiny Pro — $4.99, no subscription.
  2. Select all your product photos at once from the file picker.
  3. Compress and download each file, ready to upload through Shopify's normal product image uploader.

Why local processing matters

Compression happens on your own device using your browser's canvas API. Your product photos are never sent to QuickTiny's servers — only the one-time PayPal payment involves a server, and that's for billing only, not your images.

Free tool vs. Batch Pro

For a single photo, the free Image Compressor works the same way at no cost. Batch Pro is for when you have many images to get through — a new collection, a full catalog refresh, or a batch of photos from a shoot — and don't want to compress them one by one.

Frequently asked questions

Does this integrate directly with Shopify?

No. QuickTiny doesn't connect to your Shopify store or claim any official affiliation with Shopify. You compress your images here, then upload the resulting files yourself through Shopify's normal media uploader.

What happens to transparent PNGs, like product cutouts?

Batch Pro exports JPG files, which don't support transparency. Any transparent areas are filled with white before compression, so a transparent product cutout will end up on a white background.

Does this resize the image dimensions?

No. Compression reduces file size at the same width and height — it doesn't crop or resize your photos.

Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?

You can select as many images as your browser can comfortably handle in one batch. Very large batches (hundreds of high-resolution photos at once) may be slower since everything runs on your device.

Is this a subscription?

No. QuickTiny Pro is a single one-time payment of $4.99 that unlocks batch compression permanently in that browser.
