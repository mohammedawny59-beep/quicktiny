What happens

  1. Transcribes every word on your device (Whisper).
  2. Scores every possible clip for a strong hook, a complete thought, pace and energy — then picks the best ones that don’t overlap.
  3. Tracks the speaker’s face so the 9:16 crop keeps them centred, smoothly.
  4. Cuts pauses, adds animated captions, a hook title and a progress bar — and exports each clip as MP4 with sound.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free with no watermark?

Yes — unlimited clips, no sign-up, no watermark.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Transcription, face tracking and rendering all run in your browser. Only the AI models are downloaded once.

How does it pick the clips?

It looks for moments that open with a hook (a question, a number, a strong claim), finish a complete thought, keep a lively pace and stay within the length you chose. You can rename, preview and skip any clip.

Which videos work best?

Talking videos — podcasts, interviews, lectures, tutorials and livestreams. Clear speech and one main speaker give the best results.
