How to put it on your video

  • Download the MP4 with a green screen (or blue if your video has a lot of green).
  • In CapCut: Overlay → add the clip → Remove background → Chroma key → pick the green. In Premiere / DaVinci use Ultra Key / 3D Keyer.
  • Use 16:9 for YouTube and 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts so the overlay sits in the right place.
  • Pick a colour that matches your brand and reuse it everywhere for a consistent channel look.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free with no watermark?

Yes, unlimited free downloads with no watermark.

Can I get a transparent background?

Videos with transparency aren't supported by most phone editors, so the tool exports a clean green or blue screen that every editor can key out in one click.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic text runs right-to-left with proper letter joining, and the layout mirrors itself for Arabic.

Are the YouTube or Instagram logos included?

No. The overlays use neutral designs, so you can use them on any platform without trademark issues.
