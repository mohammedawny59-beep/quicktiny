    Is it safe to paste a real token?
    
    
    Decoding and verification run entirely in this page with the Web Crypto API — the token, secret and keys are never sent anywhere, and nothing is stored. Still, treat production tokens like passwords: prefer test tokens, and never paste a private key into any website.
    

    
    Frequently asked questions
    
    
    Is a JWT encrypted?
    No. A normal JWT (JWS) is only Base64URL-encoded and signed — anyone holding it can read the payload. The signature proves who issued it and that it wasn't changed. Encrypted tokens (JWE) have five parts and can't be read without the key.
    
    
    Where do I find the public key?
    Most identity providers publish it at /.well-known/jwks.json (the jwks_uri in their OpenID configuration). Paste the whole JSON — the right key is picked by the token's kid.
    
    
    Why is my token “expired” when it just worked?
    Check your computer's clock — expiry is compared to your device time. Servers usually allow a minute or two of clock skew.
