How a weighted grade is calculated

  • Turn each score into a percentage: score ÷ out of × 100.
  • Multiply each percentage by its weight and add them up: Σ (percentage × weight).
  • Divide by the total weight. If the weights add up to 100%, that's your final grade; if they add up to less, it's your grade so far.
  • Final exam needed = (target − grade so far × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight.

Frequently asked questions

What if my weights don't add up to 100%?

The calculator divides by the total weight you entered, so with only some items graded you get your current average. It shows the weight total so you can spot a typo.

How do I calculate my grade so far?

Fill in the items you already have and leave the rest blank. The result is the weighted average of the graded items.

How much do I need on the final to pass?

In the final-exam box, enter your current grade, how much the final is worth and the grade you need (e.g. 60). The answer is the minimum final score.

Is anything uploaded?

No. It all runs in your browser and your rows are saved only on this device.
