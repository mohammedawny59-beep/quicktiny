UTM Builder — Campaign Links That Keep Your Analytics Clean

Build UTM-tagged links for Google Analytics in seconds — or a whole set at once, one per channel. Built-in naming checks catch the mistakes that split your reports, like "Facebook" vs "facebook". Turn any link into a QR code with one click.

Build a link

    Pick every channel you'll post this campaign on. Each link gets the right source and medium, sharing your campaign name.

    What each UTM parameter means

    • utm_source — where the visitor came from: instagram, newsletter, google.
    • utm_medium — the type of channel: social, email, cpc, qr.
    • utm_campaign — the campaign name: summer_sale, eid_2026.
    • utm_content — which ad, button or version: video_a, banner_top.
    • utm_term — the paid keyword. utm_id — a campaign ID for cost imports.

    Naming rules that keep reports clean

    • Always lowercase — Google Analytics is case-sensitive.
    • No spaces — use _ or -.
    • Pick one spelling per channel and reuse it. The builder warns you if a new value is close to one you used before.
    • Don't add UTM tags to internal links on your own site — they restart the session.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do I see the results?

    In Google Analytics 4: Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, then switch the dimension to Session source / medium or Session campaign.

    Do UTM links hurt SEO?

    No, as long as your pages have a canonical tag (most sites do). Use UTM links in ads, emails, social posts and QR codes — not in internal navigation.

    Is my data stored?

    Only in your own browser (the recent-links list), so you can reuse names. Nothing is sent to a server.
