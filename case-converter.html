Case Converter — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case

Paste your text, pick a case, and QuickTiny converts it instantly — no retyping needed.

How to use Case Converter

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Click UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case.
  3. Click Copy to copy the converted text.

Key benefits

  • Four case styles in one tool.
  • Instant conversion, no page reload.
  • Runs locally in your browser.
  • Handy for headlines, names and formatting cleanup.

Privacy

Case Converter runs entirely in your browser using local JavaScript. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to QuickTiny's servers.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word. Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence, like normal prose.

Does this handle punctuation correctly?

Sentence case capitalizes the letter after sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ?) as well as the start of the text.

Is there a text length limit?

No practical limit — conversion happens instantly in your browser regardless of length.
