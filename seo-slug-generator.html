SEO Slug Generator — Title to URL Slug

Type a page title or headline and QuickTiny generates a clean, lowercase, hyphen-separated URL slug — ready to paste into your CMS.

How to use SEO Slug Generator

  1. Type or paste a title into the input field.
  2. The slug updates live below it.
  3. Click "Copy slug" to copy it to your clipboard.

Key benefits

  • Lowercases text and replaces spaces with hyphens automatically.
  • Strips accents and special characters that break URLs.
  • Updates as you type — no button needed to generate.
  • Free, unlimited, no account required.

Privacy

SEO Slug Generator runs entirely in your browser using local JavaScript. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to QuickTiny's servers.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good URL slug?

Short, lowercase, hyphen-separated, and descriptive of the page content — avoid stop words and special characters where possible.

Does this tool remove accented characters?

Yes, accented letters are normalized to their base form (e.g. é becomes e) so the slug stays URL-safe.

Will it handle non-English text?

It works best with Latin-alphabet text. Non-Latin characters (like Arabic or Chinese) will be stripped since they aren't standard in URL slugs.
