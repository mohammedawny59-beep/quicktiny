Instagram Story Size: 1080 × 1920 px

Instagram Stories and Reels are 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16). Drop any image to resize it — keep the subject in frame with the focal point, or fit the whole photo on a blurred background — and check the safe zones hidden by Instagram's buttons.

Story safe zones

Keep text and faces out of the top ~14% (profile and progress bar) and the bottom ~20% (reply box and buttons). The red overlay shows exactly where.

The same 9:16 size works for TikTok, Facebook Stories and WhatsApp Status, which are pre-selected too.

Quick answers

What's the best format for Stories?

JPG for photos; PNG for graphics with text.

What makes it better than a basic resizer

  • Focal point: click the face or product once and every crop — square, story, banner — keeps it in frame.
  • Blurred-background fit: show the whole photo in a different shape without black bars, like Instagram does.
  • Safe zones: see which parts of a Story, Reel or TikTok are hidden behind the app's buttons before you post.
  • 17 sizes, one ZIP: named by platform and size, ready to schedule.
  • Private: everything happens in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Are these the official sizes?

They're the sizes each platform recommends for upload as of 2026. Platforms change layouts often, so check their help pages for new formats.

JPG, PNG or WebP?

JPG for photos (smallest), PNG for graphics with text or transparency, WebP for websites.

Will it upscale small images?

Yes, if a size is larger than your original, but upscaled images look softer. Start with the largest original you have.
