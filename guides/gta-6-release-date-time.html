GTA 6 Release Date, Time and Platforms — What’s Confirmed

GTA 6 launches Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here’s only what’s confirmed, with sources — plus a live countdown and a launch-day checklist.

What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar hasn’t announced an unlock time, or whether it’s a single global moment or midnight per region — so any exact time you see now is a guess. The GTA 6 Zone countdown counts to midnight on 19 November in your time zone and will be updated when the official time is out.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not at launch. A PC version hasn’t been officially announced; dates floating around are unofficial reports.

Get ready for launch day

  • Start the pre-load now if you bought digitally, and free at least 212 GB — check your space.
  • Online play on consoles normally needs PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass Core or above.
  • Creating content? Use the AI Clip Maker and gameplay → vertical for Shorts.
  • Tick off the launch checklist — it’s saved in your browser.
  • Streaming? Grab the free OBS kit: a starting-soon screen, countdown and live banner.

Does it run on Xbox One or PS4?

Not on PS4. On Xbox One only through Xbox Cloud Gaming, using Series S settings — quality depends on your connection. Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox.
