How to Remove a Green Screen in CapCut

Got a subscribe button, lower third or caption clip on a green screen? Here’s how to lay it over your video in CapCut on phone and desktop — and fix green edges.

Updated: 2026-11-11

On your phone

  1. Open your project with your main video.
  2. Tap Overlay → Add overlay and pick the green-screen clip.
  3. Stretch it to fill the frame, tap it, then Remove BG → Chroma key.
  4. Drag the colour picker onto the green, raise Intensity until it disappears and add a little Shadow to soften edges.

On desktop

  1. Drag the green clip onto a track above your video.
  2. In the Video panel open Remove BG and enable Chroma key.
  3. Pick the green with the eyedropper and adjust strength and shadow.

Fixing common problems

  • Green fringe around text: nudge intensity or shadow up.
  • Parts of your video vanish: your footage contains similar green — export the overlay on a blue screen.
  • Overlay in the wrong place: export it in the same format as your video (9:16 for Reels, 16:9 for YouTube).

Free green-screen overlays

Motion Titles (lower thirds, subscribe button, countdowns), Animated Captions and Paper Motion Studio all export green or blue screen MP4s — free and without a watermark.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapCut support transparent video?

Most phone editors don’t, which is why a clean green screen is the most reliable option.

Green or blue?

Green by default; blue when your footage has green clothes or plants.

Does this work in InShot or Premiere?

Yes — InShot has a Chroma tool for picture-in-picture clips, and Premiere uses Ultra Key.
