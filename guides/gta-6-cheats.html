GTA 6 cheats: are there cheat codes?

GTA 6 is out today, 19 November, and Rockstar still hasn’t published any official cheat codes. The lists already circulating are unconfirmed. Here’s the official status, how cheats worked in GTA 5, and what to know before trying any code.

Updated: 2026-11-19

Launch-day status (19 November)

Rockstar hasn’t published any GTA 6 cheat codes — not on the Newswire, its support site or the official game site. Some sites are already posting full lists with counts and descriptions, but these are unconfirmed: no official source backs them, and many are copied from GTA 5 or made up. We’ll update this page once codes are confirmed by Rockstar or reliably tested in-game.

How cheats worked in GTA 5

  • Consoles: a quick sequence of button presses during play.
  • In-game phone: numbers you dial on your character’s phone.
  • PC: a console where you type the cheat’s name.
  • Cheats worked in story mode only, not online.

Before you use any cheat

  • In GTA 5, using cheats disabled trophies and achievements for that session. Expect something similar if GTA 6 has cheats.
  • Don’t overwrite your main save after experimenting — use a separate slot.
  • Avoid “free cheats” sites that ask you to download software or log in with your account — they’re scams.
